# Employee Handbook

Full-time employees receive 25 vacation days per year. Unused days can be carried over until the end of March.

Remote work is allowed up to three days per week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Finnish time.

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of purchase through the expense portal. Receipts are mandatory.

Laptops are replaced every three years. Request a replacement through the IT helpdesk.
